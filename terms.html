CubeTimer Community Guidelines

Version 1 · Effective 17 October 2026

These guidelines cover CubeTimer's optional Sharing feature: your display name, profile photo, friend requests, the weekly leaderboard and the daily challenge. You agree to them when you create a Sharing profile, and the app asks you again whenever they change. If you do not agree, do not use Sharing; the timer and everything else in the app keep working without it.

There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. Offending names and photos are removed, and the accounts behind them can be deleted without warning.

Not allowed

In your display name, your profile photo, or anything else you send through Sharing:

  • Sexual or pornographic content, or nudity.
  • Hate speech, slurs, or attacks on people because of their race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability or any other identity.
  • Harassment, bullying, threats, or encouraging anyone to hurt themselves.
  • Graphic violence, or promoting violence, terrorism or extremist groups.
  • Anything illegal, or promoting illegal drugs, weapons or gambling.
  • Pretending to be CubeTimer, its developer, Apple, or another person.
  • Spam, advertising, or personal information such as phone numbers and addresses, yours or anyone else's.
  • Results you did not get fairly, such as made-up times on the leaderboard or the daily challenge.

How names are checked

Every new display name is checked on the server against a list of words that are not allowed, including common misspellings and look-alike characters. A name that matches cannot be saved. The check cannot catch everything, which is why reporting exists.

What you can do

  • Block someone from their row in your friends list or from a friend request. Blocking removes the friendship and stops them sending you requests. They are not told.
  • Report someone from the same places, choosing a reason such as an offensive name or photo, harassment, or spam, and adding a note if you like. They are not told who reported them.
  • Remove a friend at any time.
  • Email huangdgm@gmail.com about anything the app does not let you report.

What happens to reports

I review every report within 24 hours. When content breaks these guidelines I remove it, and I delete the account of anyone who posts objectionable content or harasses other users. I may also act on content that nobody has reported. If your name or photo is removed and you think that was a mistake, email me.

Changes

If these guidelines change in a way that matters, the version number above goes up and the app asks you to agree again before showing you anything shared.

Contact

Dong Huang · huangdgm@gmail.com

CubeTimer 社区准则

第 1 版 · 生效日期：2026 年 10 月 17 日

本准则适用于 CubeTimer 的可选“共享”功能：你的显示名、头像、好友请求、每周排行榜和每日挑战。创建共享资料时即表示你同意本准则；准则变更时，App 会再次请你确认。如果你不同意，请不要使用“共享”功能；计时器以及 App 的其他功能不受影响。

我们对不当内容和滥用行为零容忍。违规的显示名和头像会被移除，相关账号可能在不另行通知的情况下被删除。

禁止的内容

在你的显示名、头像，或通过“共享”发送的任何内容中，不得包含：

  • 色情、淫秽内容或裸露内容。
  • 仇恨言论、侮辱性称呼，或基于种族、民族、国籍、宗教、性别、性取向、残障或任何其他身份对他人的攻击。
  • 骚扰、欺凌、威胁，或鼓动他人伤害自己。
  • 血腥暴力内容，或宣扬暴力、恐怖主义、极端组织。
  • 任何违法内容，或宣传毒品、武器、赌博。
  • 冒充 CubeTimer、其开发者、Apple 或其他任何人。
  • 垃圾信息、广告，或电话号码、住址等个人信息（无论是你的还是别人的）。
  • 非正当取得的成绩，例如在排行榜或每日挑战中伪造的时间。

显示名如何审核

每个新的显示名都会在服务器上与一份禁用词列表进行比对，包括常见的变体拼写和形近字符。命中的显示名无法保存。自动审核无法拦截所有内容，所以我们同时提供举报功能。

你可以做什么

  • 拉黑：在好友列表中对方那一行，或在好友请求中，都可以拉黑对方。拉黑会解除好友关系，并阻止对方再向你发送请求。对方不会收到通知。
  • 举报：在同样的位置可以举报对方，选择理由（如不当显示名或头像、骚扰、垃圾信息），并可附上说明。对方不会知道是谁举报的。
  • 删除好友：随时可以删除好友。
  • 邮件联系：App 内无法举报的问题，请发邮件至 huangdgm@gmail.com。

举报如何处理

我会在 24 小时内处理每一条举报。对于违反本准则的内容，我会将其移除，并删除发布不当内容或骚扰其他用户的账号。即使没有人举报，我也可能对违规内容采取措施。如果你的显示名或头像被移除而你认为有误，请发邮件联系我。

准则变更

如果本准则发生重要变更，上方的版本号会增加，App 会在向你展示任何共享内容之前请你再次确认同意。
