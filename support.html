CubeTimer Support

Speedcubing timer, CFOP practice and optional AI coaching

Need help? Use Me → Feedback in CubeTimer, or email huangdgm@gmail.com. Please include the app version, your device and a description of what happened. Screenshots are helpful; remove personal information you do not want to share. Never send passwords or verification codes.

My practice history is not appearing on another device

Use the same Apple Account for iCloud on both devices, enable iCloud Drive and allow CubeTimer to use iCloud. Open CubeTimer on both devices while connected to the internet. Check Me → Privacy & Data → iCloud sync for its status. Offline changes can sync after the device reconnects; this may take time. Avoid deleting the app or clearing data while investigating missing history.

Signing in to Sharing is separate from iCloud. Sharing sends weekly totals to accepted friends; it does not transfer your full practice history between devices.

I purchased Pro, but it has not unlocked

Check that Media & Purchases uses the Apple Account that made the purchase. In CubeTimer, open Me → Privacy & Data → Subscription status and choose Restore subscription. Complete any Apple verification on your device. If it still does not unlock, send the app version and a screenshot of the subscription status, with personal details removed.

How do I cancel or request a refund?

Apple manages payment and renewal. On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → CubeTimer to manage the subscription. Deleting CubeTimer or deleting a Sharing account does not cancel a subscription. For eligible refund requests, use Apple's Report a Problem; Apple decides eligibility.

How does AI coaching work?

The app's standard coaching findings are calculated on your device. Optional AI weekly plans require Sign in with Apple, your explicit agreement to send a training summary, and Pro or an eligible trial offered in the app. Plans follow a weekly cycle; an active AI plan is not replaced every time you open the coach. Check the review date shown on the plan.

The AI receives a training summary, not your individual practice solves or scrambles. You can withdraw the agreement in Me → Privacy & Data. AI can make mistakes. Use the plan's report link or contact email if something is unsuitable. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I delete records or my account?

You can delete individual solves in history and learning attempts in their history. Diagnostics have a delete action in their options menu. Read each confirmation before proceeding. These changes can sync to your other devices through iCloud.

Me → Privacy & Data includes deletion of all solve data. This is different from deleting your Sharing account: manage that separately in Sharing. Feedback is submitted separately; email its reference number if you want to request its deletion. The privacy policy explains the scope of each action.

What do the practice timer and scrambles check?

CubeTimer does not inspect a physical cube or automatically verify that you performed a case correctly. Use the displayed preparation moves when practising a specific CFOP case. Practice scrambles use random moves and are not official WCA random-state scrambles.

CubeTimer 支持与帮助

魔方计时、CFOP 练习与可选 AI 教练

需要帮助时，可在 CubeTimer「我的 → 意见与反馈」提交，或发送邮件至 huangdgm@gmail.com。请说明 App 版本、设备和遇到的问题。可以附截图，请先遮盖不希望分享的个人信息；不要发送密码或验证码。

另一台设备看不到练习历史

两台设备应使用同一个 iCloud Apple 账户，开启 iCloud 云盘并允许 CubeTimer 使用 iCloud。保持联网，并在两台设备上打开 CubeTimer；可在「我的 → 隐私与数据 → iCloud 同步」查看状态。离线修改会在恢复网络后同步，可能需要一些时间。排查历史缺失时，请先不要删除 App 或清空数据。

共享登录与 iCloud 同步是两回事。共享只向已接受的好友展示周汇总，不负责在设备之间传输完整练习历史。

购买了 Pro，但尚未解锁

请确认「媒体与购买项目」使用的是购买订阅的 Apple 账户。在 CubeTimer「我的 → 隐私与数据 → 订阅状态」点击「恢复订阅」，并在设备上完成 Apple 要求的验证。如果仍未解锁，请提供 App 版本及遮盖个人信息后的订阅状态截图。

如何取消订阅或申请退款？

付款和续费由 Apple 管理。请在 iPhone 或 iPad「设置 → 你的姓名 → 订阅 → CubeTimer」管理订阅。删除 CubeTimer 或删除共享账号不会取消订阅。退款可通过 Apple「报告问题」申请，是否符合条件由 Apple 决定。

AI 教练如何工作？

标准教练分析在设备上计算。可选的 AI 周计划需要通过 Apple 登录、明确同意发送训练摘要，并拥有 Pro 或符合 App 内显示的试用条件。周计划按周期执行，已有 AI 计划不会在每次打开时重新生成；请查看计划上的复盘日期。

AI 收到的是训练摘要，不包含你的单条练习成绩或打乱。你可在「我的 → 隐私与数据」撤回同意。AI 可能出错；计划不合适时，可使用计划中的问题报告入口或邮件联系。详情见隐私政策。

如何删除记录或账号？

计时历史和学习历史支持删除单条记录，诊断的选项菜单也有删除入口。操作前请阅读对应确认提示。删除会通过 iCloud 同步到你的其他设备。

「我的 → 隐私与数据」提供删除全部成绩数据的入口。它与删除共享账号不同，后者需在共享功能中单独处理。意见反馈独立提交，如需申请删除，请邮件提供反馈编号。各操作的范围见隐私政策。

练习计时和打乱会自动检查魔方吗？

CubeTimer 不会识别实体魔方，也不会自动验证公式是否执行正确。练习指定 CFOP 情况时，请先使用页面的准备步骤。练习打乱采用随机转动，不是 WCA 官方随机状态打乱。
